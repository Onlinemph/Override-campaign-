▸ How to run a turn
Step advances time by one tick. Run until event fast-forwards and pauses at the next thing worth your attention (a new contact, a battle, an ending).
Clock tiers (picked automatically): WATCH = 6-hour jumps when nothing's near, PULSE = 1 hour, CONTACT = 6-minute turns once forces are close.
Contact ladder: GHOST (something's there) → SHADOW (size & heading) → CONTACT (composition) → LOCK (full roster; artillery/air can target it).
EMCON: DARK (silent, off-net, hard to find) · PASSIVE (normal) · ACTIVE (radar blazing — sees far, seen far).
Status log colors: dimmed = routine · gold = battles & endings · amber = warnings (off-net, cut supply) · red = losses. Tick show detail for the full dice-by-dice firehose.
When a battle triggers the campaign freezes: resolve evasion, or export the handoff, fight it on the table, and paste the result back in.

Campaign:
Session-zero checklist
① Pick the campaign above (or build one in the editor).
② Agree a default battle resolution mode — Full BattleTech, Alpha Strike, or Quick Resolution (GM roll-off) — and any house rules.
③ Confirm victory conditions (VP threshold / end day) — shown in the bar above.
④ Send each player their private link from the Player links panel; they only ever see their own side.
⑤ Restart with --log file.jsonl if you want the session saved (see the header indicator).
⑥ Start the clock with Run until event; ↶ Undo step rewinds if you advanced too far.
