      ▸ Reading your screen
      

        Contacts climb a ladder as you detect more: GHOST (something's there,
        ±1 hex) → SHADOW (size & heading) → CONTACT (composition) →
        LOCK (full roster). They fade a rung each hour you don't re-detect.

        "X old / late" is how stale the intel is — a report travels at courier speed
        and only arrives when its sender is on-net, so you may be seeing the past.

        ON-NET formations take new orders instantly; OFF-NET ones run on their
        last orders and conditionals until they're back in a command node's reach.

        EMCON: DARK (silent, off-net, hard to find) · PASSIVE (normal) · ACTIVE
        (radar blazing). A conditional lets you pre-plot a reaction for when you
        can't reach a formation live.
